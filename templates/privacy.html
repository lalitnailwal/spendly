Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a password (stored in hashed form). As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to keep the service running reliably.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve Spendly: to log you in, display your expenses and summaries, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal and expense data, except where we are required by law to retain it.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third party services to operate, such as Google Fonts for typography and hosting providers for infrastructure. These providers may receive limited technical information, such as your IP address, when you use the site. We do not share your expense data with them.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
